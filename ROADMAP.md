XmlCraft is a clean-room, open-source XML editor in the spirit of Altova XMLSpy, built in Rust with egui, like its sibling [PdfCraft](https://github.com/storytold/pdfcraft). The first release (0.1.0) is an XML editor with XSD and DTD support, validation, schema generation from XML, and sample XML generation from schemas. JSON and YAML follow.

## Headline numbers

- **Feature breadth: 6.6% shipped** (10 shipped and 58 partial of 152 counted features; 64 P0, 48 P1, 25 P2, 15 P3; 10 out of scope), 25.7% with partial counted half, from `cargo xtask parity`. The editor opens, edits, colours and saves XML files byte-faithfully; validation against a DTD and an XML Schema, Entry Helpers from either, Assign Schema, and the generators (schema inference, sample XML, with their dialogs) work; the bundled schema set, the downloads, quick fixes and JSON/YAML are not there yet.
- **W3C xmlconf** (XML 1.0 fifth edition, validating, namespace-aware): not-well-formed **100%** (1017 of 1017), valid **100%** (721 of 721), invalid **100%** (227 of 227; the baseline is empty). W3C **xsdtests** (XML Schema 1.0, 2007 release, accepted tests): **98.23%** (37,754 of 38,436; schemas 96.21%, instances 99.34%; 682 known failures are the baseline).

- 2026-10-30: M2.4, the download and the cache (the user approved the new network access). - 2026-10-28: M2.3 (first part): `xmlcraft-resolve` (L3, no I/O, wasm-clean), the resolution chain. `Resolver::resolve` takes a reference (a DTD part's public and/or system identifier, or a schema URI or namespace, with the location of the document that has it) and looks in the open documents, the user's catalogs, the bundled catalogs, and then at the location itself: a bundled file (also by a relative name from another bundled file), a file or `file:` URI, or an `http`/`https` URL through the cache and then the network. Downloading follows a policy (on by default; offline; bundled schemas on or off): an `http` URL is tried as `https` first, a copy is current for 30 days and is then asked about with its ETag / Last-Modified, a server that cannot be reached leaves the old copy, served as stale, a copy from the future is not current, and a download may have 10 MiB; one load has a budget (256 resources, 64 MiB). Every place looked in is kept in a `Trail`. Files, open documents, the HTTP client, the cache and the clock are traits the caller implements (`MemoryCache` and `FixedClock` are included): the client and the cache on disk are M2.4, the wiring into the DTD loader and the `schema_resolve` tool come with them. 27 tests of the chain against a world made of maps, property tests of the path and URI arithmetic, and 24 mutations of the new code, each caught by a test.
- 2026-10-28: M3.2 XSD datatypes. The new `xmlcraft-datatypes` crate (L1, no I/O, wasm-clean) has the 45 built-in types, defined by facets as the specification defines them (`integer` is `decimal` with `fractionDigits` 0 and a pattern, `NCName` a `Name` that matches a pattern, …), the twelve facets, list and union types, and `SimpleType::validate`: white space is normalised, the patterns are matched against the lexical form, the lexical form is mapped to a `Value`, and the facets of every derivation step are checked against it, with a `Violation` that names the validity constraint it breaks (`cvc-datatype-valid.1.2.1`, `cvc-pattern-valid`, …). Values: decimals of any size, float and double (`NaN` equals `NaN`), the eight date and time types in the seven-property model (chrono does the calendar; years of up to 18 digits are moved by whole 400-year cycles when they leave chrono's range; `24:00:00`; no year 0; the order of values with and without a time zone is partial, and is checked against chrono for instants), durations (equal by months and seconds, ordered over the four reference dates, so `P1M` and `P30D` are in no order and `P1Y` is longer than `P364D`), `hexBinary`, `base64Binary`, `anyURI`, `QName` and `NOTATION` (namespace and local part), and lists. `restrict`, `list_of` and `union_of` make types and refuse what the schema component constraints refuse: a facet that does not apply, a value that is not valid for the base type, `minLength` over `maxLength`, a facet that loosens the base type's, a `fixed` facet that changes, both inclusive and exclusive bounds, bounds that leave no value or cannot be compared; all the mistakes are returned. Tests: every type's lexical space, the dates and durations, the facets and derivations, names, and properties (nothing panics, canonical forms read back, the orders agree with chrono and with integer arithmetic). 78 mutations of the new code were each caught by a test.

First set, from W3C (licence W3C-20150513, its text kept): the XML Schema schema, DTD and datatypes DTD, xml.xsd, and the XHTML 1.0 Strict, Transitional and Frameset DTDs with their three entity sets (10 files). A document is dirty when its text state differs from the saved one (undoing back to the saved text is clean again) or its encoding does. `Session` holds the documents (opening an open file shows it, also when reached by another path to the same file; closing shows the neighbour). `xmlcraft-platform` (native only) reads only regular files, with a size limit that also holds while reading, and a stamp taken from the open handle that is read again if the file changed meanwhile; it writes atomically through `craft-atomic` (no staging file left; a symbolic link, even a dangling or chained one, keeps its link) and notices a file changed or removed on disk (size, modification time, and on Unix the inode change time and file identity, so a same-size write that restores the time is seen). The shell wiring (tabs, the editor, Open/Save, unsaved-changes prompts, drag and drop) is next.
