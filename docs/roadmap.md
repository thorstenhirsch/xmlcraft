## Exit criteria (MVP path)

- **M0:** `cargo xtask ci` green on Ubuntu and Windows; a dry-run release builds, installs and launches on all six targets (win x64/arm64, macOS universal, linux x64/arm64).
- **M1:** editor performance targets met (1 MB: keystroke-to-paint p95 under 8 ms; 100 MB: opens in under 3 s, scrolls at 60 fps); xmlconf not-well-formed 100% of the tests that need no DTD (the rest closes in M2, gap 16); byte-identical open/save round-trip property holds; an hour of fuzzing finds no panic.
- **M2:** xmlconf not-well-formed, valid and invalid all 100% (met for the applicable tests with M2.9: the invalid tests are counted, not compared with their section); DocBook 4.5 and XHTML documents validate offline; at least 99% verdict agreement with `xmllint --valid`.
- **M3:** xsdtests at least 90%; at least 98% agreement with `xmllint --schema` on the curated set; a 10 MB document validates in under 1 s.
- **M4:** every corpus document validates against both its inferred XSD and DTD; at least 95% of generated samples validate; xsdtests at least 95%; 0.1.0 released for all six targets (met: v0.1.0, 2026-11-04).
- **M5:** the JSON-Schema-Test-Suite and the yaml-test-suite are fetched by `cargo xtask conformance` and ratcheted (every draft listed above; YAML 1.2 events); a BSON file and its Extended-JSON view round-trip to identical bytes when nothing was edited; a 10 MB JSON file opens, colours and validates within the budgets of M1/M3.
- **M6:** every pair of the four formats converts and, where the mapping is lossless, converts back to an equal document (property tests); the Grid edits and saves any format through the same edits as the text view; the three-way merge of a base and two edits that do not touch the same node never loses either edit.
- **M7:** QT3 (XPath/XQuery) and the W3C XSLT 3.0 suite are fetched and ratcheted (XPath 3.1 at least as good as `xee` alone, XQuery 3.1 and XSLT 3.0 measured and stated); a breakpoint stops a transformation, an XPath and an XQuery at the line it names; the profiler's hit counts equal a counted run.
- **M8:** every OpenAPI version validates against its official JSON Schema (the official examples pass); a SOAP 1.1 and a SOAP 1.2 call to a loopback mock service round-trips and the debugger holds, edits and forwards a message; the browser view renders a fixed fixture with JavaScript off and the network blocked.
- **M9:** the XRechnung test suite of KoSIT (UBL and CII) gives the expected verdicts; an XBRL 2.1 conformance subset is ratcheted; editing the XML of a ZUGFeRD PDF leaves every byte before the update unchanged, the file re-parses, and an external PDF/A checker still accepts it.
- **M10:** xsdtests at least 99.5% with the baseline of known failures shrinking (gap 21: Derivation Valid, `xs:redefine`, UPA, counters); a 100 MB file opens, scrolls and is analysed within the M1 budgets (gaps 17 and 18); an edited file is recovered after a forced kill and a change on disk is noticed while the window is unfocused; text in CJK, Arabic, Hebrew and Indic scripts draws without boxes (glyph-coverage test); the accessibility snapshot lists every control of the main window; the update check and single instance work in their kittest and CLI tests.
- **0.2.0** is released when M5–M10 have no task left and every exit criterion above is met.

## Decisions that shape the roadmap

- Own clean-room XSD 1.0 and DTD engine (no third-party validator at runtime): one grammar model feeds validation, completion, Entry Helpers and sample generation.
- Standard schemas are bundled (about 150 files, hard cap 250) *and* downloaded and cached on demand.
- JSON, YAML and BSON follow the MVP; YAML and BSON are validated with JSON Schema because they have no schema language of their own. The Altova XMLSpy documentation is the reference for what each feature includes; e-invoicing and PDF-embedded XML are not XMLSpy features and are designed from the public standards.
- The XPath, XQuery and XSLT engine is a vendored, patched `xee` extended with debugging and profiling hooks and an XQuery front-end (decision of 2026-11-05); the PDF handling is an own minimal crate that only ever appends an incremental update.
- Full network clients and listening debugger proxies are in scope, started by a person (`AGENTS.md` §4).
- The first release (0.1.0) shipped with the Windows builds unsigned and the macOS app only ad-hoc signed (no certificate, no notarisation); signing arrives when the owner provides the secrets (docs/releasing.md ▸ Signing).
